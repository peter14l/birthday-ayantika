I

Before anything else

Ayantika,

I keep meaning to say this properly, so I am putting it somewhere I cannot take back.

You have a way of making ordinary things worth noticing. A walk with no destination. A conversation that starts nowhere and ends at two in the morning. The particular quiet of sitting beside someone and not needing to fill it.

This is not for a birthday, or a date on a calendar. There is no occasion. It is just a place for the things I would otherwise only remember to say too late.

Baban

IV

The small things

Four of them, out of more than four.

  1. i

    The half-second before you laugh, when your face has already decided.

  2. ii

    You keep believing out loud, even on the days I have stopped.

  3. iii

    You notice the things everyone else walks past, and then you do something about them.

  4. iv

    The face you make when you think no one is watching.

V

A short chronology

In the order I remember them, which is not the order they happened.

  1. Adhyapan

    The first time I saw you. I spent the whole period trying to look like I wasn’t looking.

  2. Saraswati Pujo

    A school evening, a bad joke, and an embarrassment I still replay on purpose.

  3. The first hug

    Brief, awkward, and completely disarming.

  4. Your hand

    You reached first. I have been steady ever since.

  5. The first date

    We were meant to be somewhere. We ended up walking instead.

VI

Anyway,

Thank you — for the messages I read twice, for the patience I have not always earned, and for being the kind of person who makes an ordinary Tuesday feel accounted for.

No occasion. No end date.

Baban
